Our Mission

Why CivicEye Exists

Bangladesh's city corporations serve large urban populations yet rely on manual, paper-based systems. Issues go unreported. Reports disappear. Citizens lose trust in government.

CivicEye replaces this broken chain with a structured digital workflow — every complaint tracked, every decision logged, every taka of budget accounted for publicly.

When citizens can watch their complaint move through the system in real time, trust in local government grows. That is the mission.

Roles & Responsibilities

7 distinct roles — each with precisely defined responsibilities and scoped access. Department Admins may be designated as Department Heads by the Super Admin.

Citizen

Public

Reports issues, tracks status live, rates resolutions. Can submit anonymously.

Surveyor

Specialist

Inspects complaints on-site, submits formal reports with itemised cost estimates.

Field Worker

Labour

Physical infrastructure work: roads, water, sanitation, parks, fire, health, building.

Technician

Technical

Technical systems: electricity, street lights, traffic signals, IT, CCTV.

Dept. Admin

Management

Scoped to one department. Assigns surveyors, requests transfers, dispatches workers.

Dept. Head

Head

Designated department head. Receives incoming transfer notifications and leads the department.

Finance Officer

Finance

Reviews and approves expense requests, allocates annual budgets, monitors spending across all corps.

Super Admin

System

User and department management, budget allocation, transfer approvals, system-wide reporting.
