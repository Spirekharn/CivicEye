Who uses CivicEye?

Six distinct roles — each with a purpose-built experience and clearly scoped access.

CITIZEN

Citizens

Report infrastructure issues, upload photos, track status live, and rate completed resolutions.

SURVEYOR

Surveyors

Inspect assigned issues on-site, submit formal reports with itemised cost estimates.

WORKER

Physical repair crew for roads, water, sanitation, parks, fire, health, and building.

TECH

Technicians

Specialists for electricity, traffic signals, IT infrastructure, and CCTV networks.

ADMIN
